Waterproofing services

Residential, commercial and industrial work. We find out why it is leaking first, then pick the treatment.

Terrace waterproofing

Crack filling, surface preparation and multi-coat membrane systems for RCC terraces and slabs.

Roof leakage repair

We trace where the water is actually coming in and repair it, instead of just painting over it.

Bathroom waterproofing

Treatment of toilets and wet areas to stop seepage into walls and the floor below.

Wall seepage treatment

Damp interior walls and exterior seepage treated with injection and waterproof coatings.

Basement waterproofing

Basements, lift pits and sumps protected against groundwater pressure.

Water tank leakage repair

Overhead and underground tanks repaired and coated with suitable materials.

Expansion joints

Joint cleaning, filling and sealing with polysulphide and PU sealants.

Epoxy tile grouting

Chemical anchoring

Chemical anchors and rebar fixing for structural additions and heavy fixtures.

Exterior coatings

Crack-bridging waterproof coatings for outer walls and parapets.

Wholesale materials

FOSROC Brushbond RFX
